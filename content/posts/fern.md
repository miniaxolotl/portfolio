## A Different Kind of Photo Album

Fern is a private photo-sharing app from Bramble Labs Inc. Create a capsule for a trip, a celebration, or an ordinary weekend. Invite your people, choose the collection window and reveal time, and gather everyone's perspectives in one place.

The defining constraint is simple: contributed photos stay hidden until the reveal, even from the host. A capsule cover sets the scene without giving away what people have added. When the time comes, the group opens the album together.

## Before, During, and After

The experience is organized around three states. Before the collection window, you set up the capsule and invite people. During it, members contribute from their camera or photo library. After the reveal, they can look through the shared album, react, comment, and save favorites.

Those states give the product a clear rhythm. A shared collection is useful, but the anticipation is what makes Fern feel different from another folder of photos.

## Building Across the Stack

The mobile app uses Flutter for iOS and Android. A Hono/Bun API and background worker support the application, while a separate Next.js website introduces the product and collects launch interest.

The website's interactive capsule is an illustration of the experience, not a live shared album. That distinction matters: it gives visitors a way to understand the reveal without requiring an account or pretending the app is already publicly available.
