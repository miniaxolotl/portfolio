## A Home for the Work

[Bramble Labs Inc.](https://bramblelabs.co) brings together three products with different jobs: Fern for shared photo capsules, Grove for private AI-agent memory, and Burrow for public HTTPS tunnels to localhost.

The company site gives them a shared home. It needs to make the relationship clear while giving each product enough room to explain itself. Fitr is a separate venture under Fitr Technologies Inc.

## A Family, With Individual Identities

Fern's flower mark, Grove's two-leaf sprout, and Burrow's arch each carry into the product websites and marketing assets. Bramble's botanical identity connects the family. Warm backgrounds, expressive serif typography, and clear product illustrations give the sites a common visual language.

The portfolio uses those same approved assets. The artwork is editorial: it introduces a product's idea, while the live product sites provide the current experience, availability, and documentation.

## A Small, Focused Website

The company website uses Next.js, React, and TypeScript, with locally hosted DM Sans and Instrument Serif. Its content is server-rendered, with a responsive navigation and links to the individual products.

It is intentionally a public introduction. The products keep their own application infrastructure, account flows, and documentation. The company page helps people discover the work and find the right next step.
