# Portfolio brand artwork

These assets are optimized WebP derivatives of the Bramble Labs marketing
library at `/home/elias/Development/bramble-labs/marketing`, selected on
2026-11-10 from its `website-graphics` edition (2026-11-05). The originals are
retained in that library; proportions and identities are unchanged.

For each of `fern`, `fitr`, `grove`, and `burrow`:

- `*-banner.webp`: `<brand>/website-graphics/banner.webp` (1920×720), resized
  to 1280×480
- `*-mark.webp`: `<brand>/avatars/<brand>-avatar-accent-400.png`, resized to
  160×160

These are editorial website compositions, not application screenshots. Grove
uses the selected two-leaf sprout identity documented in `grove/brand/README.md`.
Its older cover with the retired Vercel URL is not used.

Fern's banner photography (road trip, campfire, and sparklers) comes from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) images credited in the marketing
library's `website-graphics/manifest.json`. Fitr's garment cutouts are
user-supplied photographs from its website. The Grove and Burrow illustrations
are original marketing-library artwork.

The bundled DM Sans and Instrument Serif files come from the same library's
`_source/assets/fonts/`. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included alongside the
fonts in `src/app/fonts/`. The portfolio pages use system sans-serif typography;
the generated sharing image uses the bundled DM Sans regular and bold files.

Bramble Labs Inc. is the company behind Fern, Grove, and Burrow. Its website is
linked from the About section; the portfolio no longer carries Bramble Labs
artwork or a studio card. Fitr is independently operated by Fitr Technologies
Inc.
