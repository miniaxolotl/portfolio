# Portfolio brand artwork

These assets are optimized WebP derivatives of the supplied
`/home/elias/Development/marketing` library, selected on 2026-10-19.
The originals are retained in that library; proportions and identities are unchanged.

For each of `fern`, `fitr`, `grove`, `burrow`, and `bramble`:

- `*-banner.webp`: `<brand>/google-display/images/<brand>-clean-01-1200x628.png`
- `*-mark.webp`: `<brand>/avatars/<brand>-avatar-accent-400.png`, resized to 160×160

Grove uses the selected two-leaf sprout identity documented in
`grove/brand/README.md`. Its older cover with the retired Vercel URL is not used.
These are editorial campaign compositions, not application screenshots.

Fern's friends photograph comes from
[Unsplash](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1529156069898-49953e39b3ac), under the
[Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license). Fitr's garment cutouts are
user-supplied photographs from its website. The Grove, Burrow, and Bramble
illustrations are original marketing-library artwork.

DM Sans and Instrument Serif are self-hosted from the same library's
`_source/assets/fonts/`. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included alongside the
fonts in `src/app/fonts/`.

Bramble Labs Inc. is the company behind Fern, Grove, and Burrow.
Fitr is independently operated by Fitr Technologies Inc.
